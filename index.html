Transaction Compliance Monitoring

Team lead

Python, PyOD (CBLOF), scikit-learn, SHAP, pandas

Why: fraud is rare and its patterns change, so labeled examples are scarce, and reviewers need to see likely fraud first.

Compared three unsupervised anomaly-detection methods (CBLOF, K-Means and Local Outlier Factor) on credit-card transactions, and chose CBLOF. I then added rule checks for high-value, round and zero amounts and measured their value: the rules added 46 flags and no extra fraud, so the review queue is ranked by anomaly score, with rule hits kept as reason tags. Each flag is explained with SHAP (via a surrogate model, out-of-bag R² 0.84) and logged with its score, rule tags, top reasons, model settings and timestamp for reviewer traceability.
